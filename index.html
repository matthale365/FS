FamilySearch Indexing Helper

A free Chrome extension that takes the repetitive clicks out of attaching records on FamilySearch. It filters your search results, remembers the people you look up, and fills in forms for you.

Add to Chrome Free · Opens the Chrome Web Store

What it does

Once installed, it works on its own whenever you're on FamilySearch. Click the extension icon to turn features on or off.

🔍

Auto Filter

Hides search results that are already attached to the tree, so only records that still need work are left. A bar above the results shows how many were hidden, with "Show all" to bring them back. Records linked to two different tree people stay visible with a warning, since that usually means a duplicate.

💾

Saves people automatically

When you open a record on FamilySearch, a Find a Grave memorial or an Ancestry page, the person's name, sex, birth and death details are saved. The extension keeps your last 4 people, and you can also save a page by hand from the extension menu.

✍️

Fill from saved

A "Fill from saved" button appears on the Add Person form and the Alternate Name popup. Pick a saved person and the form fills in, with dates matched to FamilySearch's standard format. With Auto Fill on, it fills in the most recent person as soon as the form opens.

➕

Add Unconnected Person, up top

Adds a large "Add Unconnected Person" button to the top of the recents menu, so you don't have to scroll to the bottom to find it.

Getting started

It takes about a minute.

  1. Click "Add to Chrome" above, then "Add to Chrome" again on the Web Store page.
  2. Pin it by clicking the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar and the pin next to FamilySearch Indexing Helper.
  3. Open FamilySearch and work as usual. The helpers show up on their own.
Used the old bookmark tools? The extension replaces the Filter, Add New Person and Auto Find bookmarks, so you can delete them once it's installed.
Privacy: Saved people stay in your browser and are cleared when Chrome closes, so the next person on a shared computer starts fresh. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
